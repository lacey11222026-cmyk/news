CORE ACTIVITIES

The Center of Excellence for Energy Efficiency (CoE) focuses on the following key activities:
1. Providing high-quality technical support: Conducting surveys, assessing energy efficiency potential, performing energy audits following international standards, and providing financial and technical cost-benefit analysis to help businesses make investment decisions.

2. Disseminating materials: Sharing and disseminating technical guidelines, technological catalogues on efficient energy use developed within the DEPP3 framework, and valuable documents from other stakeholders. We also provide updates and research findings related to the development and revision of the legal framework for efficient energy use.

3. Training and Capacity building: Organizing training sessions, workshops, and experience-sharing events on implementing energy efficiency projects in the industrial sector, capacity-building for Departments of Industry and Trade and relevant departments, industrial enterprises, energy efficiency consulting firms, experts, and related training institutions.

4. Networking: Connecting stakeholders to promote investment projects for energy efficiency in the industrial sector.

TARGET AUDIENCE

The Center of Excellence for Energy Efficiency (CoE) is designed to serve the following key groups:

Industrial enterprises

Especially key energy-consuming businesses looking for feasible and long-term energy efficiency solutions.

Local governmental authorities on efficient and economical energy use

Departments of Industry and Trade and other relevant departments.

Consulting firms and Energy efficiency service providers

Organizations and individuals offering consulting services such as consulting firms, industrial promotion centers, local energy conservation consulting centers, universities, and research institutes.

Sponsors and Financial institutes

Donors, financial institutions, commercial banks, industrial enterprises, technology solution providers, and energy-saving equipment suppliers.

Learn more about DEPP3 Program through: https://depp3.vn/
